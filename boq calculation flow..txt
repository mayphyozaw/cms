BOQ Calculation Flow 
Step 1: Measurement Entry (your "detail measurement UI")

User inputs:

Length, Width, Height
Formula type (area, volume, etc.)

 System calculates:

Quantity
Step 2: BOQ Item
work_scope_id
description
quantity (from measurement)
unit (m2, m3, kg, etc.)
unit_price

System calculates:

amount = quantity × unit_price

Step 3: BOQ Total

Group by:
Category (optional)
Location (floor, zone)

System calculates:

total_amount = SUM(all item amounts)

7. Advanced (Real Industry Practice)

You can extend BOQ with:

Wastage
Adjusted Qty = Qty × (1 + wastage%)

Tax
Final = Total + VAT

Discount
Final = Total - Discount
