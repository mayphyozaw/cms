Drawing Measurement vs Site Measurement
    Drawing Measurement => for estimation & BOQ     => Before construction
                                                       Used for estimation
    
    Site Measurement    => for actual work & claims =>  Actual work done
                                                        Used for payment
    
    
    Drawing Measurement
    ↓
    BOQ Estimate
        ↓
    Construction Starts
        ↓
    Site Measurement
        ↓
    Actual Progress Claim
        ↓
    Final Account
