Concrete Volume (Wet Volume) 
1 CFT ရဖို့ Dry Material  = 1.54 CFT

Dry Volume Factor = 1.54 

Dry Volume Formula
==================
Dry Volume = Wet Volume * 1.54


Material Quantity
================
Material = Dry Volume * (Material Parts / Total Parts)

Cement      =    1.54 × (1/7)
            =    0.22 CFT


Sand        =    1.54 × (2/7)
            =    0.44 CFT


Aggregrate  =    1.54 × (4/7)
            =    0.88 CFT


Example 
Concrete Work = 40CFT (Wet Volume)
Mix           =  1 : 2 : 4

Dry Volume    = Wet Volume * 1.54
              = 40 * 1.54
              = 61.67 CFT

Cement Volume      = Dry Volume * (Material Parts / Total Parts)
                   = 61.67  * (1/7)
                   = 8.8 CFT
    
Sand Volume       = Dry Volume * (Material Parts / Total Parts)
                  = 61.67  * (2/7) 
                  = 17.6 CFT
       
Aggregrate Volume  = Dry Volume * (Material Parts / Total Parts)
                   = 61.67  * (4/7) 
                   = 35.2 CFT

1m3 = 35.3147 CFT

1 Cement Bag = 1.25 CFT
